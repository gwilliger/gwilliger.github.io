HOMEWORK TO HAND IN ON PAPER:
Problems are listed as chapter.problem thus 1.2 is Chapter 1, problem 2. Starred problems (announced after homework is collected) are graded for being correct and can get bonus points. *You must show your work for any math.*

*Give traceable references/citations (URLs) for any information you use not from the textbook/lectures, cite using
quotation marks, indicate paraphrasing and give sources for numbers you give (even if from the textbook/lectures).*

 You are expected to write at college level. If there are too many errors in basic grammar, spelling, capitalization or punctuation, points
can be taken off. If you get stuck and don't know how to approach a problem, say where you got stuck. 
That's perfectly OK for doing some effort.  If you have no idea where to begin, say so, so that we can work to get you
on track.

The point of homework is to  train for tests, so even if you hand in homework and say you don't know,
you're expected to ask for help at some point, even after the fact.  With all this said, honesty will be rewarded.
It's better to be honest and say you don't know than to copy/plagiarize and get written up for it.

All questions are the property of either the textbook authors (Jay Pasachoff and Alexei Filippenko) or Gerard Williger.
Copyright Gerard Williger, University of Louisville, 2026.
It is strictly forbidden to post them to any third party, and especially for profit by any person or company,
or to upload them to any artificial intelligence facility (which essentially publishes them openly, without credit).

You do not have to copy the questions onto your homework.   You can write the problem number or name,
that is enough. Remember: you must SHOW YOUR WORK AND ALL CALCULATIONS (illustrating how you
got your answer) to get credit.
You must also cite where you got your answers (chapter.section.subsection in the
textbook or slides, or outside sources). These instructions apply to ALL homework throughout the semester.
If you quote directly/verbatim, use quotation marks.  You can earn regular, full credit with direct quotes.  However,
you cannot earn extra credit using any direct quotes for a given problem.
